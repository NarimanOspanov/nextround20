Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how the anti-cheating actually works.

AI overlays are the eyes and ears. They run as a desktop app on top of everything else. Even in a clean virtual machine, an overlay still works — because for the end user the information is delivered through system audio and the image on the screen, which no process check can see.

At first glance it seems simple, but the downsides and risks are significant. For candidates, being asked to install software on a personal device sends a bad signal and creates security risks. For companies, it raises real reputation and legal concerns. Our web-based approach removes all of this — no installation, no privacy issues, and no legal complications.

NextRound combines keyboard-only signal tracking — tab switching, window switching, copy/paste, and other shortcuts — with multiple-monitor detection. Most importantly, we found an innovative way to eliminate the risk of AI overlays completely, with no desktop software to install.

Test it yourself by running a mock interview. As the candidate, try one of the popular cheating tools like Cluely during a test round — NextRound detects and prevents the attempt in real time. For comparison, try the same thing on a standard Zoom or MS Teams call and you'll see the overlay work unchecked. That contrast is exactly why NextRound exists.
